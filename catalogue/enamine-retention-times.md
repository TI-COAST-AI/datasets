# Enamine — LC–MS Retention-Time Prediction Study

A study of machine-learning-based retention-time prediction for
routine LC–MS analysis, using internal Enamine measurements.

Catalogue status: research source identified; access to the
underlying experimental dataset remains unconfirmed.

## Source

[ChemRxiv paper](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-0rplb)

[Published article](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c00514)

The abstract was verified through publication metadata.
The full paper and supplementary files could not be inspected.

No public download of the internal Enamine dataset has been
verified for this catalogue.

## Contents

The abstract reports:

- Internal Enamine LC–MS data used for retention-time modelling.
- A graph-neural-network model based on GATv2Conv+DL.
- Evaluation of the selected architecture using METLIN SMRT.
- Application to a 120-second LC–MS method.

Dataset size, downloadable file formats, molecular identifiers,
and detailed experimental metadata remain unverified.

## Suggested uses

- Study graph-neural-network approaches to retention-time prediction.
- Discuss applications of predictive models in routine LC–MS.
- Compare research approaches using the separately available
  METLIN SMRT dataset.

Training directly on the internal Enamine measurements depends
on establishing access to those data.

## Data quality and preparation

Distinguish the internal Enamine training data from the METLIN
SMRT data used to evaluate the selected architecture.

METLIN SMRT is already represented in this catalogue. Do not
count its reuse in this study as an independent dataset.

Performance reported for a particular LC–MS method should not
be assumed to transfer to other chromatographic conditions.

Before reusing any released files, inspect molecular identities,
retention-time units, method details, duplicates, and the
training/test split.
